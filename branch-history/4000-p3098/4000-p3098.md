## Rationale

Implement P3098, "Contracts for C++: Postcondition captures": with
`--[no_]contracts_p3098` (implied by `--contracts_p3850` unless given,
enabling contracts unless `--[no_]contracts` is given, an error in C mode),
a postcondition can have a capture list before its operand,
`post [n = size()] (size() == n + 1)`, and
`__cpp_contracts_postcondition_captures` is predefined as `202609L` (GCC's
value).

A capture is `name = initializer` or the name of a parameter (as
`name = name`).  The capture list's tokens are cached with the operand
(`a_contract_specifier::capture_token_cache`) and scanned before it, in the
contract scope: every initializer is scanned before any capture is declared
(`scan_postcondition_capture_initializer`), so it sees the parameters and
not an earlier capture; its type is deduced as for `auto` (decayed), the
initializer converted to it as an argument passed by value is, with the
destructor of a copied class checked for access and deletion.  The captures
are then declared, hiding the parameters in the predicate, where they are
not const (`set_postcondition_captures_in_scope`).  They are variables owned
by the specifier (`a_contract_specifier::captures`), like the result name:
on no scope's list, copied to file scope with their initializers and walked.
`this`, `*this`, capture defaults, captures by reference, duplicates, a
simple capture that names no parameter and capture lists on preconditions
and `contract_assert` are diagnosed with GCC's messages; a pack capture is
diagnosed as not yet supported (EDG-76).  A constructor's captures name
members only through an explicit `this`, as its preconditions do.

Two declarations of a function give the same captures, as written
(`equiv_postcondition_captures`, "mismatched postcondition captures in
declaration"), and their predicates are compared with corresponding captures
paired (`equiv_postcondition_predicates`).  In constant evaluation the
captures of each postcondition whose semantic is not ignore are allocated
and initialized with the preconditions, in lexical order, and released once
the postcondition is evaluated; a capture whose initializer is not constant
makes the assertion not constant, and so, for now, does a capture whose type
has a nontrivial destructor (EDG-77).  The C++-generating back end prints
`post [x = (e)] (...)`, and g++ (given `-fcontracts-p3098` by edg_gxx.sh)
checks the captures at run time.  In the checks the front end generates for
the C-generating back end (1100-p2900-base-lowering's prologue and epilogue)
each capture is a variable of the definition, initialized in the prologue
with the preconditions' checks, in declaration order, from a copy of its
initializer (`make_contract_capture_inits`; a class object made in a
temporary is elided, a copy of the temporary's initialization,
`copy_contract_capture_init`, initializing the variable), and named by the
postcondition's copied predicate in place of the capture
(`set_contract_copy_captures`).  Where an initializer might throw, the
initializations are in a try block whose handler reports a violation of kind
post_capture, and a flag set once they are done guards the postcondition's
check and the captures' destruction.  Captures with a nontrivial destructor
are destroyed by explicit destructor calls at the end of the epilogue, in
reverse order, and, when an exception leaves the body, by the handler of a
try block around the body (`protect_contract_captures`, at the end of the
body, moving its lifetimes as a coroutine's body is moved; a
function-try-block is first put in a block), which rethrows -- except for a
constructor's or destructor's function-try-block (EDG-87).

Tests: `contracts/p3098/` (constant evaluation under quick_enforce and
ignore, errors, checks of capture initializers, redeclarations, the macro,
the syntax without the option, a parameter used only in a capture,
captures through edg-gxx on members, templates, classes and lambdas, and the
run-time checks of captures under both configurations) and
133 GCC and Clang imports in `contracts/imported_p3098/`.

## Compile gap

Code of earlier bands already handles captures, and refers to what this
commit defines: 1040-p2900-base-parse's `scan_contract_specifier_operand`
and `rescan_contract_specifier_operand` take the capture list's token cache
and call `scan_postcondition_captures` and
`set_postcondition_captures_in_scope`, and its callers (with
1080-p2900-base-sema-templates') remove the captures with the result name
(`remove_contract_operand_symbols`); expr.h declares the three expr.c
functions.  1060-p2900-base-sema-core's `match_contract_specifiers` compares
predicates with `equiv_postcondition_predicates`, and `compare_expressions`
pairs captures through `corresponding_compared_captures`.
1070-p2900-base-sema-predicate-scope's `contract_predicate_makes_const`
exempts the captures, `check_function_contract_predicate` checks a
constructor's capture initializers with `find_implicit_this_use_in_capture`
and `mark_contract_params_used` walks them.
1090-p2900-base-sema-constexpr's `evaluate_function_contracts` initializes
and releases the captures (declaring the three functions defined here).
1020-p2900-base-il's printing of contract specifiers prints captures.
1100-p2900-base-lowering's `prepare_contract_checks_for_lowering` makes the
captures' variables (`make_contract_capture_inits`, `if_contract_flag`,
`set_contract_copy_captures`), and its `contract_copy_param_for_proxy`
substitutes them; its `a_contract_violation_call_kind` has the post_capture
case.  1040-p2900-base-parse's `scan_function_body` calls
`protect_contract_captures`.  All of these are defined here.
