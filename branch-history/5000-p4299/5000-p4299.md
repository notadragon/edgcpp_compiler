## Rationale

P4299, "Contracts for C", in C++ only (M14a; contracts in C are not
supported, EDG-60): with `--[no_]contracts_p4299` (an error in C; passed to
the front end and g++ by the drivers as `-f[no-]contracts-p4299`), `_Pre`,
`_Post` and `_ContractAssert`, P4299's spellings for C, are alternative
spellings of `pre`, `post` and `contract_assert` when contracts are enabled.
As in GCC and Clang, the option does not enable contracts, is not implied by
`--contracts_p3850`, and predefines no macro.

`_ContractAssert` is a second keyword for `tok_contract_assert`, and `_Pre`
and `_Post` are keywords of their own (`tok_p4299_pre`, `tok_p4299_post`),
as Clang has them (DECISIONS.md E28 in notadragon_wg21), so none of the
three can be used as an ordinary name; GCC still reads `_Pre` and `_Post` as
identifiers spelled `pre` and `post`.  Where a function contract specifier
may begin (`curr_token_starts_function_contract_specifier`,
`curr_token_starts_labeled_contract_specifier`),
`demote_p4299_contract_keyword` makes such a keyword the identifier `pre` or
`post`, which the contract specifier code recognizes, and
`cached_token_is_contract_intro` accepts them in cached tokens; so members,
templates, lambdas and P3400 labels need nothing else.  cp_gen_be puts a
template's tokens out as written, and g++ gets the option.

The tests are `tests/tests/contracts/p4299/` and GCC's and Clang's
`p4299-in-cxx` in `tests/tests/contracts/imported_p4299/` (their
`underscore-keywords-without-p4299` were imported with the base facility,
GCC's `p4299-c-is-terminating-noexcept` with P4298).
