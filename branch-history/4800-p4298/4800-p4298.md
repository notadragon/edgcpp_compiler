## Rationale

P4298, "Nonthrowing Evaluation Semantics" (M13a): with
`--[no_]contracts_p4298` (implied by `--contracts_p3850` unless given, and
enabling contracts; an error in C; passed to g++ by the drivers as
`-f[no-]contracts-p4298`), and `__cpp_contracts_nonthrowing_semantics`
202609L, as our GCC predefines it.

Two evaluation semantics, `ces_noexcept_observe` and `ces_noexcept_enforce`,
act as observe and enforce but terminate the program when the violation
handler exits with an exception.  `--contract_evaluation_semantic` accepts
`noexcept_observe` and `noexcept_enforce`; without the option each is its
throwing variant, with GCC's warning, decided once all options are read.  A
P3595 configuration, which already parsed the two names, now gives them
(`supported_semantic`), without the warning it gave for them, and the
default entry carries them by name rather than by the enum's position.  In
constant evaluation no exception can leave a handler, so
`contract_semantic_for` gives enforce and observe there, as GCC does.

A P3400 label may allow and compute the two semantics: their
compute_semantic results are probed too
(`a_contract_specifier::label_computed_noexcept_semantics`, initialized,
displayed), and with the option the semantic is chosen by
`apply_contract_label_facets_p4298`, with GCC's best fit
(`contract_semantic_best_fit`): by level -- ignore, observe and
noexcept_observe, enforce and noexcept_enforce, quick_enforce -- the
configured semantic's level first, then upward, then downward, preferring at
each level the variant of the configured semantic's kind.  The library's
values (6 and 7, with 5 being P3100's assume) are mapped by a table rather
than the facets' "EDG value plus one".

Through edg_gxx.sh g++ implements the semantics at run time.  The
C-generating back end still accepts only ignore and quick_enforce (the
semantics that call the handler arrive there with M13b).

The tests are `tests/tests/contracts/p4298/` and the imports in
`tests/tests/contracts/imported_p4298/`.
