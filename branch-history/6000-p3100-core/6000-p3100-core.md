## Rationale

P3100, "Implicit contract assertions" -- its hook into the configuration of
contract assertions only (M15a, DECISIONS.md E15): the front end makes no
implicit contract assertions; through edg_gxx.sh g++ makes them in the C++
it compiles.

* `--[no_]contracts_p3100` (implied by `--contracts_p3850` unless given, and
  enabling contracts; an error in C; `-f[no-]contracts-p3100` to the front
  end and g++ through the drivers) predefines the P3100 macro of the
  compiler emulated, `__gcc_contracts_p3100` or `__clang_contracts_p3100`,
  as 202609L; libstdc++ defines `__cpp_lib_contracts_implicit` by it.
* `--[no_]contracts_allow_assume` (not implied, not enabling contracts; an
  error in C) predefines `__gcc_contracts_allow_assume` or
  `__clang_contracts_allow_assume`, and lets the assume evaluation semantic
  be chosen: `--contract_evaluation_semantic=assume`, a configuration's
  `"assume"`, a group semantic, and an assertion-control label's facets.
  `ces_assume` is a new evaluation semantic; `contract_semantic_for` gives
  `ces_ignore` for it once a label has had its say, so constant evaluation
  and the C-generating checks (which allow it, as ignore) see ignore: the
  predicate is not evaluated, as in GCC.
* Without the option, assume is replaced silently by its best fit (E29.1):
  ignore, or under a label the next allowed semantic up, as GCC's
  `contract_semantic_best_fit` does.
* With it, a label's facets are resolved by
  `apply_contract_label_facets_p3100`: assume is at the level below ignore,
  and `compute_semantic` is probed for assume too
  (`label_computed_assume_semantic`).

The tests are `tests/tests/contracts/p3100/` and the imports in
`tests/tests/contracts/imported_p3100/`.
