## Rationale

One IL entry kind, `iek_contract_specifier` (`a_contract_specifier`), serves
all three kinds of contract assertion: it holds the kind (pre, post or
assert), the predicate, a postcondition's result-name variable, the
position of the introducing keyword and the predicate's text (the comment
of a violation).  A routine's preconditions and
postconditions are a list on `a_routine::contract_specifiers`, in
declaration order; a `contract_assert` is a new statement kind,
`stmk_contract_assert`, whose variant points to its specifier.  The
specifier owns its result-name variable, which is on no scope's variables
list, so the walker walks it from there.  A specifier can also hold the
tokens of its operand (`token_cache`, for the front end only, which the
walker clears): while `operand_cached` is set, for a member function's
specifier, scanned when its class is complete, and for a template's, from
which the specifiers of its instances are scanned.

The entry is walked (and so written, copied and displayed) by the generic
machinery in walk_entry.h, displayed by il_display.c, and traversed by
il_walk.c.  The C++-generating back end, a source-to-source back end that
leaves templates, lambdas and coroutines to the compiler of its output,
leaves contract assertions to it too: cp_gen_be prints a `contract_assert`
as written, and a routine's specifiers after the declarator of the first
declaration it puts out (`put_out`, a cp_gen_be-only bit on the first
specifier), each at its own position and naming the parameters of that
declaration; a lambda with specifiers always gets a parameter list.  Every
other consumer of statements treats `stmk_contract_assert` as an empty
statement until checks exist: the IL lowering turns it into one (unless
its predicate is still on it, which the IL must keep reaching) and the
C-generating back end prints `;`.  Constant evaluation of contract
assertions is 1090-p2900-base-sema-constexpr.

A reviewer should check the entry's memory region: it is allocated in the
current region, which is the region its predicate is scanned into.

## Compile gap

Nothing creates these entries until 1040-p2900-base-parse.  Until
1090-p2900-base-sema-constexpr the interpreter cannot interpret a
`stmk_contract_assert`, so a constexpr function containing one is not
constant-evaluated.
