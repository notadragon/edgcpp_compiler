## Rationale

P4451 (D4451R0, "Early Adoption of an Always-Enforced Label"), as our GCC's
`-fcontracts-p4451` (notadragon_wg21 DECISIONS.md E173, E174): with
`--[no_]contracts_p4451` (enabling contracts; an error in C; passed to g++
by the drivers as `-f[no-]contracts-p4451`), an assertion-control specifier
`< terminating >` after `pre`, `post` or `contract_assert` makes the contract
assertion a terminating one.  `--contracts_p3850` and `--contracts_p3400`
enable it, whatever the `--no_` form of the other, unless
`--[no_]contracts_p4451` is given, as our GCC does, so that the drivers' g++
agrees (the rest of the option rule, including the error for
`--no_contracts_p4451` with P3400 on, is 6995-contracts-option-rule's).

With P3400 the specifier is an ordinary P3400 label and nothing here
applies.  Without it, `terminating` is an identifier with special meaning,
never scanned as an expression; any other specifier is an error ("only
<terminating> is allowed ..."), and with neither option `<terminating>` is
pointed at `--contracts_p4451`.  A terminating assertion has no label: it is
marked `is_terminating`, and its `label_allowed_semantics` and
`label_allowed_proving_semantics` are those of P3400's
`std::contracts::labels::terminating` (enforce and quick_enforce, and with
P4405 prove), so `contract_semantic_for` chooses its semantic exactly as for
that label: the best fit (a configured ignore or observe is enforce, and
quick_enforce under the C-generating back end, which checks no other
terminating semantic).  The function's declarations must agree on the
specifier.

[basic.contract.control]'s two checks run where a label would be scanned (a
member function's when its class is complete, with the parameters in
scope): qualified lookup of `std::contracts::labels::terminating` must find
a variable, declared before that point (libstdc++ declares it in
`labels::inline __p4451` under P4451 alone), and unqualified lookup of
`terminating` must find nothing or only that entity.

The C++-generating back end puts the specifier out as `<terminating>`, for
g++.  `__cpp_contracts` is D4451R0's 202611L (or P3097's value, if
greater), and in GNU and Clang modes `__gcc_contracts_p4451`
(`__clang_contracts_p4451`) 202610L is predefined, by which libstdc++
declares the label and raises `__cpp_lib_contracts`.

The tests are `tests/tests/contracts/p4451/` and the imports of the gnu_gcc
fork's P4451 tests in `tests/tests/contracts/imported_p4451/`.
