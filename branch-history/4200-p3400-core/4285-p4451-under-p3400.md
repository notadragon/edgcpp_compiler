## Rationale

P3400 subsumes P4451: under `--contracts_p3400` the assertion-control
specifier `< std::contracts::controls::enforced >` is an ordinary label,
whose allowed set is the terminating semantics, so every program P4451
accepts means the same.  These tests run P4451's cases again under
`--contracts_p3400` alone (`-p3400`) and with both options (`-both`), and
expect the same results, or P3400's own diagnostics where P3400 reads the
specifier differently -- the lookup cases, where its `contract_control`
using-directive finds an unqualified `enforced`.  They cover run time under
a configured ignore and quick_enforce and dynamic selection, the
caller-side sequences with P3595 configurations (including a "caller"
entry) and P3097 virtual calls, constant evaluation and initialization,
redeclarations, the lookup rule, the feature-test macros
(`--contracts_p3400` implying `--contracts_p4451`), and the diagnostic for
a specifier other than `enforced` with neither option
(`p4451-diag-no-flag`).  EDG's own `p4451/r1_errors_p3400`, `r1_names`
(every accepted spelling, through g++, in all three variants) and
`run_forms` (the declaration forms under ignore and observe, and under
P3400) join them.

The `.rto` recordings and the expectations lines still show the names
before P4451R2's rename; they are re-recorded with the next contracts suite
run.

## Compile gap

None.  Pure test additions.  They need P3400's labels with their
allowed-semantics facet, P3595's configurations and P3097's virtual calls.
